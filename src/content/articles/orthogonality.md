# Orthogonality of Vectors and Subspaces

- Subspaces $V$ and $W$ are orthogonal when $v^Tw=0$ for every $v$ in $V$ and every $w$ in $W$.
- The row space of $A$ is orthogonal to the nullspace (by definition, since the dot product of every row of $A$ and $x$ is zero)
